Top 10 Pipe Brands in India 2024
1. Ajay Pipes
Ajay blog
Ajay Pipes, part of the Ajay Group, is one of the oldest names in India’s piping industry. Known for introducing plastic piping to the Indian market, Ajay offers CPVC, uPVC, SWR, and UDS pipes. Ajay Pipes stands out for its exceptional product quality, minimal complaints, and strong customer satisfaction. With six decades of expertise, it remains a preferred brand for reliable and durable piping solutions.

2. Apollo Pipes Ltd.
Apollo Pipes, a branch of the APL Apollo Group, has swiftly gained prominence in the top plumbing brands in India. The company offers CPVC, uPVC, HDPE, and PPR pipes, along with water storage solutions. Known for its extensive distribution network and large-scale manufacturing capabilities, Apollo caters to both urban and rural markets with practical, cost-effective options.

3. Ashirvad Pipes
Backed by the global Aliaxis Group, Ashirvad Pipes has become one of the top 5 pipe brands in India. It offers CPVC, uPVC, and SWR pipes, meeting the needs of residential, commercial, and industrial projects. Distributors value Ashirvad for its steady supply, excellent product performance, and extensive reach across urban and rural markets.

4. Astral Ltd.
Astral Ltd. is a leading name among the top 10 plumbing pipe brands in India. The company provides CPVC, uPVC, SWR, and HDPE pipes, along with adhesives and sanitaryware. Astral’s innovative product range and significant advertising investments have made it a well-recognized brand. Its dependable quality and strong market presence make it a top choice for both standard and specialty plumbing needs.

5. Birla HIL Ltd.
Birla HIL Ltd., part of the CK Birla Group, is a newer entrant but has quickly earned credibility. Offering CPVC, uPVC, and SWR pipes, Birla HIL caters to plumbing, sanitation, and agricultural needs. Known for affordability and reliability, it is an excellent choice for cost-sensitive customers, particularly in rural and semi-urban markets.

6. Finolex Industries Ltd.
Finolex Industries Ltd. is a household name in PVC piping, especially in agricultural applications. Its PVC-U, CPVC, and SWR pipes are trusted for their quality and affordability. Finolex’s extensive distribution network makes it a strong contender among the top pipe brands in India, particularly in rural areas where durability and cost-effectiveness are essential.

7. Prince Pipes and Fittings Ltd.
Prince Pipes is one of the top 10 pipe brands in India, offering Agri PVC, CPVC, uPVC, HDPE, and PPR pipes. With a strong distribution network and reliable quality, Prince Pipes is widely accessible and caters to diverse project needs, from residential plumbing to agricultural irrigation.

Supreme Industries Ltd. is a giant in India’s plastic manufacturing sector and a leading player in the piping industry. Its extensive product range, including PVC, CPVC, and HDPE pipes, serves residential, agricultural, and industrial sectors. With a vast distribution network and dependable quality, Supreme is a go-to brand for standardized, high-performance piping solutions.

Truflo by Hindware is an emerging name in the Indian piping market. It provides CPVC, uPVC, and SWR pipes for plumbing and sanitation applications. Wavin, part of the Orbia Group, is recognized for its diverse piping solutions, including CPVC, uPVC, and PPR pipes.
